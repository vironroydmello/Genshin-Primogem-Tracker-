Welcome! Set up in 3 steps

One pull is one wish. This app counts the pulls you’ve saved and tells you when you can afford the character or weapon you want.
  1. Enter what you have now. Type your primogems and fates under “Update your pulls” below, then tap Set balance.
  2. Set your income. Pick how you play (free-to-play, Welkin…).
  3. Add a goal. Choose a character or weapon you’re saving for.

Plan for
How are the odds worked out?
Odds on each goal use the standard pity curves (soft pity from pull 74 for characters and Chronicled, 63 for weapons) with a 55% win rate for characters, 37.5% for a chosen weapon and 50% for Chronicled. The weapon curve is a model estimate.

New here? Quick glossary
Pull
One wish. It costs 1 Fate or 160 primogems.
Primogems and Fates
Primogems are the free wish currency. Intertwined Fates are wish tickets. This app counts both as pulls.
Pity
How many pulls since your last 5★. A 5★ is guaranteed at 90 pulls (characters, Chronicled) or 80 (weapons). Odds climb from about pull 74 (63 for weapons), which is called soft pity.
50/50
A character 5★ has a 50% chance to be the featured one. If you lose, the next 5★ is guaranteed.
Welkin Moon
A monthly pass that gives 90 primogems every day.
Genesis Crystals and Starglitter
Genesis (paid) becomes primogems 1:1. 5 Starglitter become 1 fate.
Patch
A game update, about every 6 weeks. New banners arrive in phase 1 and phase 2 of each patch.
